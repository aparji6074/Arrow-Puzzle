How to play

1. Touch an arrow and it slides out in the direction of its head.
2. If another arrow is in the way, you crash and lose a heart ❤️. Lose all 3 hearts and the level ends (you can Revive).
3. Remove the arrows with a clear path first.
4. Pinch with two fingers to zoom, drag with one finger to move the board.
5. Stuck? Press Hint: the safe arrow blinks red. Press Mark to see every arrow's route (green = clear, orange = blocked, the red X shows what blocks it). Undo takes a move back. Play without accidents for 3 stars!
