Arrow Puzzle ("the app") is a puzzle game. This page explains what information the app uses.

Information we collect

The app does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (levels reached, stars, hints, sound settings) is saved only on your own device. It is not sent to us. If you clear the app's data or uninstall the app, this progress is removed.

Advertising

If the app shows ads, they are provided by a third-party advertising service such as Google AdMob. These services may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and other information to show and measure ads, under their own privacy policies. See Google's policy at policies.google.com/privacy. If the app is installed in a version without ads, no advertising data is used.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
